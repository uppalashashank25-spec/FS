FORENSIC CASE FILES

Review simulated case files to understand how different disciplines of forensics work together to solve a crime.

CASE 001 – The Locked Room

Description: A victim is found unconscious in a secure, locked room. There are no signs of forced entry.

Evidence: Broken glass shards, partial latent fingerprint on the doorknob, and a trace bloodstain.

Suspects: 3 individuals with known motives.

CASE 002 – The Missing Evidence

Description: A high-profile museum theft involving missing historical jewels.

Evidence: Muddy footwear impression near the display, stray synthetic fibers caught on a sharp edge.

Suspects: 2 overnight staff members.

CASE 003 – The Laboratory Mystery

Description: A leading scientist is found poisoned in their own research laboratory.

Evidence: An unlabeled chemical vial, a broken syringe, and wiped digital access logs.

Suspects: A rival researcher who had access to the facility.
