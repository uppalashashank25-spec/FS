CHAIN OF CUSTODY

Maintaining a chronological record of who handled the evidence is vital to ensure it is admissible in a court of law.

1. Evidence Collected

Properly documented at the scene using photography and sketches.

2. Packaged

Secured in appropriate materials (e.g., paper bags for biologicals to prevent mold).

3. Sealed

Tamper-evident seal applied with the investigator's initials and date across the seal.

4. Transferred

Logged in and physically moved to a secure evidence locker or directly to the forensic lab.

5. Laboratory Examination

Analyzed by forensic experts under controlled conditions.

6. Report Generated

Scientific findings compiled into a formal, peer-reviewed report.

7. Court Presentation

Presented as reliable, untampered evidence during legal proceedings.
