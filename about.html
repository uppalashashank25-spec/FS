ABOUT FORENSIQUEST

ForensiQuest is a comprehensive educational platform designed specifically to help students and enthusiasts understand the complex world of forensic science through interactive learning, virtual simulations, and case-based challenges.

Mission

To provide accessible, highly interactive, and scientifically accurate forensic science education to students worldwide, breaking down complex analytical concepts into understandable modules.

Vision

To bridge the critical gap between theoretical academic knowledge and practical application in real-world criminal investigations and laboratory analysis.

Key Objectives

  • Offer engaging simulated crime scene experiences that teach proper protocol.
  • Familiarize students with rigorous laboratory procedures and the chain of custody.
  • Test theoretical and practical knowledge through interactive case studies and quizzes.
  • Provide a centralized hub for forensic terminology and study resources.

Careers in Forensic Science

Forensic science is a multidisciplinary field offering various specialized career paths:

Forensic Scientist

Analyzes physical evidence like paint, glass, and drugs. Requires BSc/MSc in Forensic Science or Chemistry.

DNA Analyst

Extracts and profiles biological samples to match DNA. Requires background in Biology or Genetics.

Toxicologist

Tests bodily fluids for drugs, alcohol, and poisons to assist in determining cause of death.

Digital Forensic Analyst

Recovers hidden or deleted electronic data from computers and mobile devices.

Fingerprint Expert

Develops, lifts, and analyzes latent fingerprints using physical and chemical methods.

Forensic Anthropologist

Analyzes skeletal remains to determine age, sex, stature, and trauma.
